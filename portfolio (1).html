01

Brain Tumor Analyser

AI-powered medical imaging · February 2025
Deep Learning Computer Vision Healthcare AI
Problem

Manually scanning brain MRI slices for tumors is slow and easy to miss subtle regions — early detection matters most.

Approach
  • Preprocessed and normalized MRI scan images
  • Trained a deep-learning image-processing pipeline to flag abnormal regions
  • Generated predictions to support faster review
Result

A working analyser that highlights likely tumor regions on a scan, built as a support tool for earlier diagnosis — not a replacement for a radiologist.
